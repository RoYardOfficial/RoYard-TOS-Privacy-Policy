Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how RoYard Bot may process information when you use it. The exact information handled depends on the Bot’s features and configuration. Please review this policy alongside the Bot’s actual implementation.

Important for the RoYard owner: Before publishing, verify that the categories below match what your bot really collects, logs, stores, and shares. Remove anything that does not apply and add any additional data types, retention periods, or providers you use.

1. Information the Bot may process

To respond to commands and operate in Discord, the Bot may process Discord identifiers and interaction information, such as:

  • Discord user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, and message IDs.
  • Command names, command options, and information included in a command or interaction.
  • Server settings or preferences that an administrator chooses to configure.
  • Technical error or diagnostic information needed to maintain and troubleshoot the Bot.

Do not send passwords, payment-card details, government identification numbers, or other highly sensitive information to the Bot.

2. How information may be used

Information may be used to provide requested commands and features, remember server configuration where supported, maintain security, prevent abuse, troubleshoot errors, and improve the Bot’s reliability.

3. Storage and retention

Some information may be processed temporarily or stored if required for a Bot feature. The RoYard team should define and follow an appropriate retention period for each stored data type, and delete it when it is no longer needed for the stated purpose or when deletion is required by law.

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal information. Information may be processed by infrastructure or hosting providers used to operate the Bot, or disclosed where required by law or necessary to protect the Bot, its users, or others. Any providers with access should be limited to what is needed to provide their services.

5. Security

We take reasonable steps intended to protect information handled by the Bot. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep bot tokens and other credentials private and never share them publicly.

6. Your choices and requests

You can stop interacting with the Bot or remove it from your server. If you have a question or a request concerning information associated with your Discord account, contact the RoYard team using the community link below. We may need enough information to identify the relevant data and verify the request.

7. Children

The Bot is provided through Discord. Do not use the Bot if you are not permitted to use Discord under the rules and laws that apply to you.

8. Changes to this Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Bot changes. The current version and effective date will be posted on this page.
